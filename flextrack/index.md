# FlexTrack

Plan routines, log every set in a guided session, quick-log runs and other activities, and follow your progress and body weight over time. Everything stays on your device: there are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

- [Privacy policy](./privacy/)
- Support: [pierre.blachere@outlook.com](mailto:pierre.blachere@outlook.com)

## Common questions

**How do I back up my data?** History → Export saves every routine, session and body-weight entry as one JSON file and opens your phone's share sheet. Save it somewhere off your phone, such as Files, Google Drive or your computer.

**How do I delete everything?** Uninstalling FlexTrack removes all of its data from the device. You can also delete individual routines inside the app.

**The rest timer alert doesn't appear.** Check that notifications are allowed for FlexTrack in your phone's settings. FlexTrack asks once, the first time you start a rest.

**Does FlexTrack need an internet connection?** No. It works fully offline.
