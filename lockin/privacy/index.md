# Lock-In Privacy Policy

_Effective October 14, 2026_

Lock-In is a local-first personal planning and habit-tracking app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Your data stays on your device

Lock-In has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Plans, entries, journal text, mood history, photos, and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) never receive any of it.

## Photos

When you attach a photo to a journal entry, Lock-In copies that single image into its private storage. It does not read the rest of your photo library and never uploads anything. The app does not use the camera.

## Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. No notification content is sent to any server.

## Backups and exports

Backups, templates, and CSV exports are created only when you request them (or, on Android, written weekly to a folder you choose). Backup files are **not encrypted** and contain your journal, mood history, plan, and photos, so store and share them carefully. Where a file goes after you share it is governed by you and by the app or service you share it with.

Android system cloud backup of the app's data is disabled.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Erase data** permanently deletes everything Lock-In stores on your device, including photos and scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.

## Children

Lock-In does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
