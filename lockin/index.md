# Lock-In

Build a personal improvement plan, then track habits, metrics, goals, mood, and journal entries day by day. Everything stays on your device: there are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

- [Privacy policy](./privacy/)
- Support: [pierre.blachere@outlook.com](mailto:pierre.blachere@outlook.com)

## Common questions

**How do I move my data to a new phone?** Settings → Backup → Export backup, then on the new phone Settings → Restore backup and pick the file.

**How do I delete everything?** Settings → Erase data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.

**Does Lock-In need an internet connection?** No. It works fully offline.
