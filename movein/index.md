# MoveIn

Keep your lease and receipts together, log each room's condition with dated photos to protect your deposit, and track moving tasks and utilities. Everything stays on your device: there are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

- [Privacy policy](./privacy/)

## Common questions

**How do I back up my data?** Settings → Export backup creates one ZIP file with all your moves, photos and documents. Save it somewhere off your phone, such as Files, Google Drive or your computer.

**How do I restore a backup?** Install MoveIn, then Settings → Import backup and pick the ZIP. Importing never overwrites anything already in the app. It adds what's missing, including photos and documents missing after a phone restore.

**Photos show "not on this device".** Android's Google backup restores your records but not photo files, which are too large for it. Import your latest MoveIn backup ZIP to bring the photos back.

**Reminders don't appear.** Check that notifications are allowed for MoveIn in your phone's settings. Reminders are sent at 9:00 on the due date.
