# MoveIn Privacy Policy

_Effective October 13, 2026_

MoveIn is an app for organising a move: documents, condition photos, a checklist and utilities. It is built to work entirely on your device. **We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.**

## What MoveIn stores, and where

Everything you enter (moves, addresses, documents, photos, notes, tasks, utility details and account numbers) is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. MoveIn has no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

## Device backups

Your phone's own backup service may include MoveIn's data in _your_ backup:

- **iPhone:** if iCloud Backup is on, iOS includes MoveIn's data in your encrypted iCloud backup.
- **Android:** if Google backup is on, Android backs up MoveIn's records and settings (not photos or document files) to your Google account. Moving to a new phone with a cable or Wi-Fi transfer copies everything.

These backups belong to your Apple or Google account and are governed by their privacy policies. We have no access to them.

## Exports

When you choose "Export backup" or export a condition-log PDF, MoveIn creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, email, a messaging app, a cloud drive) is entirely your choice. We never receive it.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to photograph rooms and documents. Photos are saved only inside MoveIn.
- **Photo library:** MoveIn uses the system photo picker, so it only sees photos you choose. It reads the date a picked photo was taken, to label condition photos accurately.
- **Notifications:** to remind you about task due dates and your move-in day. These reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Files:** to attach documents you pick, and to import a backup you choose.

When photos are saved, MoveIn removes embedded metadata such as GPS location.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any document, photo, room, task, service or move inside the app. Deleting a move removes all of its data and files. Uninstalling MoveIn deletes everything it stored on the device. Copies in your device backups or in files you exported are managed by you.

## Children

MoveIn is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle children's data. In any case, it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because MoveIn collects no data, any change would apply only to how the app works on your device.
